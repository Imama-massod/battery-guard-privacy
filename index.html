Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Data Collection

Battery Guard collects no data. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or device information.

2. Local Storage

All settings and preferences are stored only on your device. They are never sent to any server or third party.

3. Internet Access

Battery Guard does not require internet access to function. No data is transmitted over the internet.

4. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions for core functionality:

  • Battery monitoring: To read battery level and state
  • Full-screen alerts: To show battery alerts over other apps
  • Foreground service: To monitor battery in the background
  • Notifications: To alert you when battery is full or low

5. Third-Party Services

Battery Guard does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
